The work

Five case studies, oldest first
01Zain Jordan · 2017–2023

Cloud gaming, before Jordan had seen it

Bringing NVIDIA GeForce NOW to a market that had never seen cloud gaming, and making it as easy to pay for as a phone top-up.

Company
Zain Jordan
Role
Product marketing, then digital products
Years
2017 to 2023
Built on
Direct Carrier Billing, value-added services

The problem

Jordanian gamers wanted PC-grade games, but PC-grade hardware is expensive and nobody had brought cloud gaming to the country. There was no proof people would pay for something they'd never tried.

What I did

  • Launched NVIDIA GeForce NOW as Jordan's first cloud gaming service.
  • Billed it through Direct Carrier Billing, so subscribing took nothing more than a SIM.
  • Grew the base through digital channels instead of retail.
  • Ran the same billing rails for a wider range of value-added services.

The result

  • 500+subscribers at launch
  • +300%subscriber growth via digital
  • 1stcloud gaming service in Jordan
02Umniah · 2023–2025

Selling ping, not megabits

Jordan's first fiber package built for gamers, sold on latency instead of speed, with a promise in writing.

Company
Umniah
Role
Digital Products Supervisor
Years
2023 to 2025
Built on
I-BRAS traffic prioritisation, live latency graphs

The problem

Every ISP in Jordan sold megabits. Competitive players care about ping, and nobody would promise them any.

What I did

  • Made latency the product: gaming traffic prioritised at the I-BRAS.
  • Built a live latency graph per game, so players could check us in real time.
  • Put the promise in writing: Low Latency or Your Money Back.
  • Bundled 24 months of free mobile cloud gaming with every tier.
  • Turned the landing page into a gaming store, with gift cards and gear next to the plans.

The result

  • 1stgaming-optimised fiber in Jordan
  • 3tiers, each with its own booster
  • 24 moof mobile cloud gaming included

03Umniah · 2023–2025

Finding where the eShop leaked

An eShop with plenty of traffic and almost no buyers, rebuilt around data instead of opinions.

Company
Umniah
Role
Digital Products Supervisor
Team
5 direct reports: 2 product owners, 3 logistics specialists
Tools
InsiderOne, Mixpanel, Hotjar

The problem

People were visiting the eShop, but very few bought, and no one could say exactly where they were dropping off.

What I did

  • Instrumented the full journey with InsiderOne, Mixpanel and Hotjar before changing anything.
  • Rebuilt the shop around what the data showed.
  • Ran the operational end too: cash-on-delivery refusals, returns and logistics.
  • Worked alongside the paid ads team to bring the right traffic in.

The result

  • 0.7→1.3%conversion rate
  • +120%internet revenue
04Umniah × Arab Bank · 2023–2025

A flagship phone, by the month

Turning a full-price phone into a monthly number people already think in, by plugging a bank's plan into the checkout.

Partners
Arab Bank, HyperPay
Role
Digital Products Supervisor
Years
2023 to 2025
Built on
HyperPay payment gateway

The problem

Flagship phones were a hard sell at full price. Paying upfront was the blocker, not wanting the phone.

What I did

  • Spotted that Arab Bank already had an instalment option its cardholders could use.
  • Adapted the HyperPay gateway so the bank could trigger a 0% plan from its side at checkout.
  • Ran a joint iPhone 16 campaign from 25 JD a month on Arab Bank cards.
  • Tied the device to a line: six months of free Elite postpaid, then an Apple Watch subsidy for staying on.

The result

  • 0%interest at checkout
  • 25 JDa month for an iPhone 16
05Etisalat by e& · 2025–now

The unglamorous middle of 5G

Turning 5G network capabilities into services a business can buy, set up and switch on without a week of manual work.
